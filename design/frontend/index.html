The Malik family

Yusuf Malik died on 3 March 2026. His daughter Sara says she's handling the estate. His wife Amina lives in the family home and uses his old phone number. Everyone and every organisation here is fictional.

    What this prototype is

    • Standalone pages with fictional data and local, simulated interactions.
    • Shared mock state in this browser, so changes in one tab show in another.
    • A design proposal for review. It isn't approved yet.

    What it is not

    • No messages, emails or calls are sent. No microphone is used.
    • No AI runs. Transcripts, readings and timings are scripted and labelled.
    • Proof results are previews, not test results.
