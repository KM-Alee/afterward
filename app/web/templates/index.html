What it does

The repetitive part, carried for you.

The family says the hardest sentence once. Afterward does the notifying, the chasing and the checking, and asks only the questions that need a person.

{{ ui.icon('chat') }}

Say it once

A voice note, a photo of a letter or a tap on a button in Telegram. No forms to learn, and nothing to repeat to each organisation.

{{ ui.icon('mail') }}

Writes to each organisation

Banks, phone companies, utilities and subscriptions get a complete, correctly referenced request, but only the ones the family has approved.

{{ ui.icon('clock') }}

Follows up for months

Replies are logged, silence triggers a follow-up, and promised refunds are tracked until there is evidence they arrived.

{{ ui.icon('shield') }}

Checks "done" against your plan

When a provider says "completed", Afterward compares it with what the family approved, and refuses to mark it done if it is not.

How it works

From one conversation to a checked outcome.

  1. 1

    Tell it once

    The family shares what they want to stop, keep and protect.

  2. 2

    Confirm the plan

    Each organisation gets a conditional plan. Anything consequential waits for an explicit approval.

  3. 3

    We write and follow up

    Requests go out through the provider's accepted channel. Replies and deadlines are tracked.

  4. 4

    Check against the plan

    A reply that does not match the approval is shown as a conflict, not as done.

{{ ui.icon('info') }}

What Afterward does not promise

Said plainly, because this is a hard time to be misled.

  • {{ ui.icon('eye-off') }}It does not promise to find every account. The list stays open, and the desk shows what was searched and what was not.
  • {{ ui.icon('hand') }}It never signs, moves money or impersonates anyone. Legal, tax and certified-document steps are prepared for a person to do.
  • {{ ui.icon('phone') }}The call room is a browser call, not a phone call. The speech check is not a guarantee.
  • {{ ui.icon('photo') }}A phone photo is kept as a photo, not a certified copy. Parts of this demo are simulated and are labelled as such.
